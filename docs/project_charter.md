# OpsPilot AI: Agentic Business Analyst Workbench for Supply Chain Operations

## Project Objective

The objective of OpsPilot AI is to simulate how a Business Analyst can use AI to support supply chain operations decision-making. The project analyzes orders, shipments, inventory, warehouses, and suppliers to identify operational KPI issues such as late shipments, stockouts, supplier delays, and warehouse utilization problems.

The project uses SQL, Python, Power BI, Jira, and LLM-based agentic workflows to convert operational findings into business requirements, Jira-style user stories, acceptance criteria, and executive-ready recommendations.

## Business Problem

Supply chain managers often have access to operational data, but they may struggle to quickly identify the root causes of performance issues and translate those findings into clear business requirements or actionable next steps.

Delays, stockouts, supplier issues, and warehouse capacity problems can affect customer experience, cost, and service levels. OpsPilot AI helps bridge the gap between raw operational data and business action by combining KPI analysis, structured LLM outputs, and Jira-style requirement documentation.

## Core Business Questions

1. Which warehouses have the highest late shipment rate?
2. Which products or categories are causing stockout risk?
3. Which suppliers are linked to longer delays?
4. Which regions have poor on-time delivery performance?
5. Which delay reasons are most common?
6. Which operational issues should be prioritized first?
7. What business requirements should be created from the findings?
8. What Jira user stories should be created for dashboard or process improvement work?
9. What recommendations should leadership consider?

## Responsible AI Controls

- AI-generated outputs will be treated as draft recommendations.
- Human review will be required before recommendations are finalized.
- Outputs will include risk levels.
- Outputs will reference supporting KPIs.
- No private or sensitive customer data will be used.
