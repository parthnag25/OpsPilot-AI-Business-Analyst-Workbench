# AI Business Analyst Workflow Documentation

## Project: OpsPilot AI

This document explains the AI-assisted Business Analyst workflow used in the OpsPilot AI project.

The workflow converts SQL-generated supply chain issue candidates into structured business analysis outputs such as issue summaries, root-cause hypotheses, business requirements, Jira-style user stories, acceptance criteria, and executive recommendations.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The purpose of this workflow is to demonstrate how AI and structured business logic can support Business Analyst work.

Instead of stopping at KPI reporting, the workflow turns operational issues into business-ready outputs that can support stakeholder review, requirements gathering, prioritization, and Jira-based delivery planning.
